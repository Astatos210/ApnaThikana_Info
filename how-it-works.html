How it works

How ApnaThikana works

Simple steps to find your home, mess, and furniture – and for partners, simple steps to fill rooms, plates, and rentals.

For Students Hostel / PG Owners Mess Operators Rental Owners

For Students

From "new in town" to fully settled – in six steps.

Create your profile

Add your college, budget, and move-in date. This helps us shortlist places that genuinely fit you.

Browse verified options

Compare hostels and PGs with real photos, clear pricing, and reviews from current residents.

Book with secure payment

Pay your deposit via UPI with written terms. Your booking is confirmed instantly.

Move in

Get your keys, complete a quick QR-based move-in, and skip the paperwork mess.

Book mess meals daily

Check today's menu, book meals, and QR check-in at the counter. Skip a meal, get a rebate.

Rent what's missing

Need a mattress, fridge, or study table? Rent by the month and return it before you leave.

Tip: keep your college name and move-in date updated – listings near you get prioritised.

For Hostel / PG Owners

Fill vacancies with verified, pre-qualified students.

Create your listing

Add rooms, photos, rent, and facilities from your phone – usually 20–30 minutes of work.

Complete verification

Upload ID and property proof. Our team verifies everything within 2–3 days.

Go live & reach students

Your listing appears for students searching near your area – including our waitlist members.

Manage bookings & deposits

Accept or decline booking requests, track deposits, and manage move-ins from the app.

Get payouts & reviews

Receive clear weekly settlements and build a reputation with genuine student reviews.

Note: commission is charged only on successful bookings – nothing for leads that don't convert.

For Mess Operators

Predict demand, cut waste, and get paid on time.

Set up plans & menus

Create monthly plans, publish daily menus, and set prices – veg, non-veg, jain, whatever you serve.

Students book meals

Students subscribe or book day-wise. You see tomorrow's demand tonight – cook exactly that much.

QR check-in at every meal

Students scan the counter QR to check in. Attendance is tracked automatically – no register, no guesswork.

Track attendance & rebates

See who ate what, apply rebates for skipped meals, and keep students happy with fair billing.

Weekly settlements

Get clear weekly payouts for the meals served – no chasing students for cash.

Tip: posting today's menu early increases bookings by a lot – students decide before hunger strikes.

For Furniture & Appliance Owners

Turn idle items into monthly income, safely.

List items with photos

Add your bed, fridge, geyser, or study table with photos, condition, and monthly price.

Set availability

Mark when each item is free – from next month or next semester. No double bookings.

Accept bookings with deposits

Students book and pay rent plus a refundable deposit through the app. Terms are written upfront.

Track returns & condition

Log returns with photos in the app. Condition disputes are resolved with clear evidence.

Refund deposits after check

Release the deposit after the return check – usually within 2–3 days. Students trust it, you stay protected.

Note: we only deduct from deposits for genuine damage with photo proof – fair for both sides.
