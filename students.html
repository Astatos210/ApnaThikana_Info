Student FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Is there any brokerage?

No. ApnaThikana never charges students brokerage. You pay the hostel or PG owner directly, and we charge the owner a small commission instead – like a listings platform, not a broker.

How do mess bookings and QR check-in work?

Open the app, check today's menu, and book a meal or subscribe for the month. At the mess, scan the QR code at the counter to check in – the operator sees your name instantly, and your attendance is tracked automatically. Skip a meal, get a rebate.

How does furniture rental deposit and return work?

You pay a refundable security deposit (typically one month's rent) via UPI when you book. On return, the owner checks the item in the app, we deduct only for genuine damage (with photo evidence), and the rest is refunded to your account within a few days.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. All payments go through UPI with receipts generated in-app. Refund policies are written on every listing before you pay, and our support team steps in if there's ever a dispute. We never ask for cash or payments outside the app.
