About us

Built by students, for students

We started ApnaThikana the year we moved to Pune – and discovered how broken student living was.

Our story

Moving to a new city shouldn't be this hard

Every year, lakhs of students move to Pune with a bag, a budget, and zero idea where to live. Brokers charge a month's rent just to show you rooms. Listings online turn out to be fake or worse than the photos. Landlords demand a full year's advance. Most freshers end up paying too much for too little – and signing contracts they don't understand.

Then there's the rest of daily life. Messes serve whatever they decide, with no menus and no way to skip a meal. A mattress, fridge, and study table cost a fortune to buy – and even more to abandon when you leave the city two years later.

We saw all of this firsthand. So we built the app we wished existed: one trusted platform where students can find a verified hostel, book mess meals, and rent furniture – without brokerage, without guesswork, without stress. ApnaThikana means "our place" – because that's what every student deserves in a new city.

Our mission

Three promises we make to every student

Trust

Every owner and property is verified. Every review comes from a real resident. What you see is what you get.

Affordability

No brokerage. Monthly furniture rental instead of heavy purchases. Clear pricing on every single listing.

Convenience

Hostel, mess, and furniture in one app. Book in minutes, move in fast, and spend your energy on college, not chaos.
